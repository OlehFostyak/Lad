Lad: ліцензії та джерела словників
==================================

Код Lad поширюється під ліцензією MIT: його можна вільно використовувати,
змінювати й поширювати, зберігаючи копірайт. Повний текст наприкінці.

Словники Lad зібрані з відкритих частотних списків слів (субтитри фільмів
і українська Вікіпедія) і поширюються під ліцензією CC BY-SA 4.0: їх можна
копіювати й змінювати, якщо вказати авторів і зберегти ту саму ліцензію.
Нижче перелічено, хто автори і що Lad змінив.

Lad нікуди нічого не надсилає: ні словників, ні набраного тексту.

2. Word lists
-------------

Contents/Resources/Dictionaries/en.lad and uk.lad are packed from the en.tsv
and uk.tsv that tools/build_dictionaries.py builds from the sources below.
They are shared under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/,
and provided as-is, without warranties, as that license states.

2.1 FrequencyWords
    By Hermit Dave, https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
    (commit 525f9b5, 2018 lists). License of the lists: CC BY-SA 4.0.
    Counted from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus of OPUS,
    http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php; the subtitles come from
    https://www.opensubtitles.org.

2.2 Ukrainian Wikipedia word counts
    wikipedia-word-frequency by Ilya Semenov,
    https://github.com/IlyaSemenov/wikipedia-word-frequency
    (commit b49c2ea, results/ukwiki-2022-08-30.txt), MIT License
    (full text at the end).
    Counted from the text of Ukrainian Wikipedia, https://uk.wikipedia.org,
    written by its contributors. The dump of 2022-08-30 is under CC BY-SA 3.0
    Unported, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/; Lad shares its
    adaptation under CC BY-SA 4.0, as section 4(b) of that license allows.

2.3 VESUM, Large Electronic Dictionary of Ukrainian (dict_uk)
    Copyright (c) 2026 Andriy Rysin (arysin@gmail.com), Vasyl Starko,
    BrUK team. https://vesum.nlp.net.ua/, https://github.com/brown-uk/dict_uk
    (release v6.8.6). License of the data: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0,
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/.
    Lad uses VESUM only to tell which counted words are normative Ukrainian
    word forms; no word or other data of VESUM is in the lists.

2.4 What Lad changed
    en.tsv, from FrequencyWords en_50k: lowercased; only words of the letters
    a-z, with one- to three-letter tokens limited to real words and common
    abbreviations; 64 contractions added with the count of a common word.

    uk.tsv, from FrequencyWords uk_full and the Wikipedia counts: lowercased,
    apostrophes unified, counts per billion subtitle words. It keeps the
    subtitle words seen at least twice, and adds at half their Wikipedia
    frequency the words Wikipedia uses at least 50 times (four letters or
    more, or with an apostrophe; no proper names). Either way, a word is kept
    only if VESUM lists it as a normative form.
