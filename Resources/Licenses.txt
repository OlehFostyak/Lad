Lad: word list sources and licenses

Lad's word lists, Contents/Resources/Dictionaries/en.lad and uk.lad, are packed from the en.tsv
and uk.tsv that tools/build_dictionaries.py builds from the data below. The data is provided
as-is, without warranties, as the licenses below state. Lad uses and shares it non-commercially.


FrequencyWords

  Word counts from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus (OPUS, http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php;
  subtitles from https://www.opensubtitles.org).

What Lad changed

en.tsv, from FrequencyWords en_50k: lowercased; only words of the letters a-z, with one-, two-
and three-letter tokens limited to real words and common abbreviations; 64 contractions added
with the count of a common word. Shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

uk.tsv, from FrequencyWords uk_full, the Wikipedia counts and VESUM: lowercased, apostrophes
unified, counts per billion subtitle words. It keeps the subtitle words seen at least twice
that VESUM lists as normative word forms; adds, at half their Wikipedia frequency, the
normative forms Wikipedia uses at least 50 times (four letters or more, or with an apostrophe,
no proper names); and adds VESUM's other forms with an apostrophe with a count of 1. It holds
material from all three sources and is shared on the terms of their licenses, non-commercially.
